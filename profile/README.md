# EmbedHarbor

**Embedded Security, SBOM & Trusted Delivery**

EmbedHarbor focuses on practical tools and reference resources for embedded security, software supply chain transparency, SBOM, and IoT cybersecurity compliance.

## CRA Readiness

We focus on helping connected-product teams understand software inventory and prepare security evidence. Readiness resources support preparation; they do not constitute certification or a determination of legal compliance.

## Trusted Delivery

Our focus is clearer software provenance, supplier SBOM interoperability, and reviewable delivery evidence. A parsed or schema-valid SBOM alone does not establish completeness, authenticity, or trust.

## Open-source Projects

Two projects are planned and undergoing preparation before public release:

- **Embedded SBOM Examples** — practical examples and guidance for embedded firmware, IoT devices, and software supply chains.
- **Supplier SBOM Test Cases** — cases for validation, interoperability, references, and delivery quality.

Source code and releases are not yet publicly available. Scope and licensing remain subject to review.
